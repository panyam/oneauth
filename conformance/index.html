Conformance

Overview of every conformance and interop suite OneAuth runs.

OneAuth runs four conformance / interop suites today. Each has its own scorecard or audit document; this page is the index.

OIDF conformance

Auto-regenerated. The OpenID Foundation conformance suite's discovery plan run against cmd/oneauth-server. Per-check FAIL/WARN outcomes diffed against known-gaps.yaml. Ratchet enforced — regressions and ratchet-ups both turn CI red.

Updated by: make testoidf

Go-native ratchet

Auto-regenerated. Cross-spec conformance fixtures (AS metadata RFC 8414, client authentication, introspection RFC 7662) written as Go tests. Same ratchet model as OIDF; pass/fail tracked against known-gaps.yaml.

Updated by: make testconformance

Keycloak interop

Hand-curated audit. Documents what tests/keycloak/ covers, what passes, and what's deliberately not covered. Updated when the test suite changes. Tracked separately from OIDF because Keycloak interop is "OneAuth validates Keycloak-issued tokens" — a different surface than "OneAuth passes OIDF cert plans."

Updated by: hand, when tests/keycloak/ changes

Authlete interop

Hand-curated audit. Documents what tests/authlete/ covers, the test client + provisioner architecture, and what remains gated on missing OneAuth surface (e.g., /authorize).

Updated by: hand, when tests/authlete/ changes

Why two flavors of "conformance"?

Spec-defined plans (OIDF) measure us against a public test plan the spec authors themselves endorse. Passing the OIDF discovery plan means we serve a valid /.well-known/openid-configuration by the OIDC working group's own definition. That's the substitutability claim — same plans Authlete, Auth0, Keycloak, Curity certify against.

Per-IdP interop (Keycloak, Authlete) measures something different: OneAuth's middleware validates tokens issued by IdP X. Useful for "you can stick OneAuth's resource-server middleware in front of an existing Keycloak deployment without changing your auth server" claims; not a substitute for spec-defined conformance.
